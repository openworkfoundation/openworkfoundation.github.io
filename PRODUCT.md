# PRODUCT.md — Open Work Foundation

## What it is

Public-interest foundation (non-profit orientation) stewarding open, auditable
labor-market infrastructure for the AI-era transition. Modeled on the Linux
Foundation pattern: neutral steward of a commons, MIT licensed, free for workers.

## Audience (priority order)

1. Influential builders/leaders (Diogo Almeida-tier): technical credibility + scale ambition
2. Funders/institutions (NGOs, governments, philanthropy): mission legitimacy
3. Workers/builders in OSS community: participation

## First surface

Public landing, EN + ES. Goal: in <60s a busy visitor gets the problem's urgency,
the evidence (neutral sources), the purpose, and one clear door to act.

## Brand commitments

- No hype, no doomerism — evidence-led urgency
- Open/auditable/public are the three semantic pillars
- MIT license, idea belongs to no one
- Anthropic cited only as a labeled source, never as truth anchor
- Bilingual EN/ES is a first-class commitment

## Mode: Persuade — visitor decides to care and act.
