Anthropic Institute

Economic Scenarios for Transformative AI

A general-equilibrium model for the US economy 2026-2030 across three bounded scenarios: modest (like the internet), substantial (50% of knowledge tasks done by AI), extreme (recursive self-improvement, knowledge employment -21.5%, labor share falling to 45.2%). Reviewed by Acemoglu and Autor.

NBER · Working Paper 32140

Applying AI to Rebuild Middle Class Jobs · David Autor (MIT)

The optimistic counter-thesis: AI as a "lever of expertise" that lets mid-education workers perform high-responsibility roles once reserved for elite professionals. If policy steers AI toward complementing workers rather than substituting them, it could rebuild the middle class.

Interactive

Econ Scenario Explorer

Interactive model built on Anthropic's scenarios plus a national survey of 10,980 Americans. Four "dials" decide how hard the adjustment hits: share of automation, creation of new tasks, occupational mobility friction, and hiring of young graduates.

Keynote · TED/Stanford

The AI Awakening · Erik Brynjolfsson

The "Productivity J-Curve": macroeconomic stats lag visible disruption because firms first spend on intangibles (process redesign, data, training). After the dip, productivity gains accelerate sharply and reward judgment, empathy and strategic creativity.
