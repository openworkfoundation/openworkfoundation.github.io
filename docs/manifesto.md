# Manifesto

## The situation

AI is eliminating knowledge work faster than the labor market can reabsorb
the displaced. This is not a prediction — it is observable. The only open
question is how orderly the transition will be.

The economy will keep growing. But growth and reallocation are different
things. GDP can rise while millions of capable people spend months or years
in the gap between the job that was automated and the job nobody could point
them to.

## The asymmetry

Labor-market infrastructure is private infrastructure. Job boards rank by
revenue. Matching is a black box optimized for the payer — the employer or
the advertiser — not for the displaced worker. The person with the most to
lose is the product being sold.

Public roads exist because no private toll road serves everyone. The same
argument applies to the infrastructure that connects people to work.

## What we build

The Open Work Foundation stewards open, auditable, free-for-workers
infrastructure for the labor transition:

- **Open protocols** — standardized skills profiles and structured job
  descriptions that any tool can read, not a proprietary pipeline.
- **Auditable matching** — transparent judgments a person can inspect and
  challenge, not an ad-ranked feed.
- **Free for workers, always.** Funded by the parties that benefit from an
  orderly transition: employers, institutions, governments, philanthropy.

## What we are not

Not a job board. Not a staffing agency. Not a think tank that only writes
reports. Not a vehicle for anyone's personal credit — the work is MIT
licensed and the institution is designed to outlive its founders.

## The model

We operate like the foundations behind the open-source commons: we host,
steward, and govern projects; we do not own the market. Code and protocols
are public. Funding is transparent. Decisions affecting the commons are made
in the open.

## The ask

This is day zero. The problem is documented, the first initiative is
designed, and the proof of concept works. What is missing is you: builders,
researchers, employers, institutions, funders.

The wave is coming whether we build or not. Build with us.
