# Initiatives

Projects hosted and stewarded by the Open Work Foundation.

## Open Talent Matcher — flagship, in design

Open, auditable infrastructure that transforms displaced workers into the
professionals AI-era jobs demand.

The concept: standardized skills profiles on one side, structured job
descriptions that employers and recruiters publish on the other, and a
transparent matching engine in the middle — where every match is a typed,
explainable judgment the candidate can inspect, not a black-box score.

- Free for candidates, always
- MIT licensed
- Designed to be replicable and forkable by governments, NGOs, and cooperatives

Status: design phase. We are looking for technical partners, pilot
employers, and funding. Open an issue to get involved.

## Job Seeker — live

[github.com/galiprandi/job-seeker](https://github.com/galiprandi/job-seeker)

An open-source, agentic job-search assistant: it searches, evaluates fit
against a declared career goal, applies, tracks, and follows up —
autonomously. It is the working proof that automation can serve the
candidate, not just the recruiter, and the seed of the Foundation's
methodology.
