# Contributing

Everything in this organization is open infrastructure: open source, open data, open ideas.
Contributions are welcome in three lanes.

## Languages

All core content lives in English (`docs/`) and Spanish (`docs/es/`). To add another
language: open an issue titled `lang: <language>` and we will scaffold `docs/<code>/`
for you to translate into. Translations land via PR, one page per commit.

## Initiative proposals

Have an idea that belongs under the Foundation? Open an issue using the
**Initiative proposal** template. A good proposal states:

- the problem it addresses and who it serves
- why it must be open/public infrastructure rather than a product
- what exists today (prototype, spec, nothing yet)
- what the Foundation (or a partner) would uniquely enable

## Articles and papers

The Articles and Sources sections are curated. To suggest a piece, open a PR that
adds it to `docs/sources.md` (bibliography-style citation + one-line summary of
why it matters). Opinion pieces also go on `docs/articles/` with the same process:
PR, one file per article, author credited.

## Norms

- English and Spanish are first-class; write in either, maintainers handle the mirror.
- No commercial self-promotion. No paywalled sources without a public mirror.
- MIT license applies to everything unless a page says otherwise.
