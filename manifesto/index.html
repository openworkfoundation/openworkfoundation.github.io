The Manifesto · First public document

The transition is inevitable.
Making it orderly is our moral obligation.

MANIFESTORead time: 60s

Artificial intelligence is eliminating knowledge work faster than the world can reabsorb it. This is not a prediction: it is already happening.

When a tool or a robot performs a role, the person does not disappear. They are left suspended between the job they lost and the one nobody can name yet. Millions of capable people will live inside that gap. Most of humanity is invited to this migration, and nobody is organizing the arrival.

GDP will grow anyway. The question is not whether there will be abundance, but who falls along the way and who distributes it.

Open Work Foundation exists to organize this transition.

We are not a company. We are not a product. We aim to be the common table: a neutral, open and auditable foundation where those who can lead the change gather. A public repository of proposals that the community examines, improves and executes. No idea is sacred, including ours.

01 /What we ask

What we ask is simple, and it demands courage:

  • 01

    Sign

    Add your name or your institution's name to this alliance. Signing is the first public act of governing this era.

  • 02

    Propose

    Bring your ideas, your evidence, your capacity to execute. Open debate is the mechanism.

  • 03

    Speak

    Put this topic on the tables where decisions are made. Awareness before the crisis is the only advantage we have.
